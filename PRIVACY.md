# Privacy policy

electrs-watch collects no data. There's no account, analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking.

The app only connects to the Bitcoin server you set up yourself, over Tor. The developer has no server,
and never sees your addresses, balances, server address, or anything else.

What the app stores, all on your phone:

- Your server's onion address, this phone's Tor key, the addresses you watch, your saved balances and
  transactions, and connection counts: in the iOS Keychain, readable only with your Face ID, and never
  in iCloud or backups.
- Your light or dark setting: in the app's preferences.

Deleting the app, or using Erase Everything in Settings, removes all of it.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/ehallmark/electrs-watch/issues.
